# Retail: what the words mean

These are the words the retail screens use. The same idea always has the same name.

## Sales

| On the screen | What it means | Other words people use |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction | One sale slip: one customer's purchase, with one or more lines | sale, receipt, bill, visit, customer, basket |
| Net revenue | Money taken, after discounts and returns | sales, takings, income |
| Average Sale Value | Net revenue divided by transactions | average basket, basket value |
| Items per transaction | How many lines are on one slip | items per basket |
| Single-item transactions | Slips with only one line | single-item baskets |
| Quantity Sold, Pairs Sold | How many pairs were sold | units |

## Cost and profit

| On the screen | What it means |
|---|---|
| Estimated Cost of Goods Sold | What the products sold are estimated to have cost: each sale line is priced with the buying price known at the time of the sale |
| Gross Profit | Net revenue less the estimated cost of goods sold |
| Gross Margin | Gross profit as a percentage of net revenue |
| Gross Profit per Transaction | Gross profit divided by transactions |
| Operating Cost | What it costs to run the branch apart from the products: salary plus daily usage |
| Net Profit | Gross profit less the operating cost. It is shown only when the operating cost is complete, otherwise a dash |

A change in a percentage is written as a plain %, for example "margin down 3.3%".

## Stock

| On the screen | What it means |
|---|---|
| On Hand Qty | How many pairs are in stock, from the newest stock count |
| Estimated Inventory Value | Quantity in stock times the buying price |
| Potential Sale Value | Quantity in stock times the selling price |
| Low stock | A product estimated to run out soon at its recent pace of sales |
| Dead stock | A product still in stock that has not sold for 90 days |
| Reorder Qty | How many to order: monthly sales times the target stock buffer, less what is in stock |
| ABC | A: the best sellers (top 80% of revenue). B: steady sellers (next 15%). C: slow movers (last 5%). N: no sales |

## Pages

- **Dashboard**: the Summary, Health, Revenue, Cost, Inventory and Customer tabs for one branch.
- **Business Alerts**: things that need a decision, such as stock about to run out.
- **Import**: Import Files, Import History and Import Health.
- **Data Overview**: the imported sales, inventory and purchases as tables.
- **Reorder Items**: what to order and how much.
- **Data Quality**: rows in the imported data that look wrong.

Severity: **Critical** needs a decision now. **Warning** needs a look. **Normal** is only for your information.

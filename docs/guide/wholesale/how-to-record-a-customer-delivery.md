# How do I deliver products to a customer?

*Other ways to ask: record a delivery, send goods to a customer, hand over an order, deliver, ship an order
out, 交货, 发货, 记录送货.*

**Short answer:** open the order, go to its **Deliveries** tab, choose what goes out, and press
**Record delivery**. You can only deliver what is **Ready**, which means allocated to that order.

## Steps

1. Open the order. The quickest way is **Today**: in the **Deliver** card press **Deliver** on the customer.
   Or press **Customers**, pick the customer and open the order, then open the **Deliveries** tab.
2. Stay on **To deliver**. At the top, check the **Delivery date**, and fill the **Delivery address** and a
   **Note** if you want (for example the bus gate it was sent through).
3. The table lists the products by place (the warehouse the products are in). For each product you see
   what is **Ready** and a field under **Deliver**.
4. Press **All** on a row to deliver everything that is ready, or choose the colors and quantities yourself.
   **Clear** takes a row back to nothing.
5. Press **Record delivery** in the page header. A message says "Delivery recorded."

## If you can't deliver

- *"Nothing is ready to deliver yet"* means nothing is allocated to this order. The products have not arrived,
  have not been counted, or are still at a gate that is not a warehouse. Items under *Not ready yet* are held
  for this order automatically as they arrive.
- You can't deliver more than the customer ordered, or more than is **Ready**.
- An older order took the stock: arrivals are shared out oldest order first.

## Afterwards

- **Delivered** (next to **To deliver**) lists what has gone out.
- A delivery by mistake: press the bin icon on its row. **Undo** appears for a few seconds.
- Record the customer's money on the **Payments** tab.

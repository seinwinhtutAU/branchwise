# How do I receive products from a supplier?

*Other ways to ask: the goods arrived, count the packages, open a package, receive stock, check what came,
start receiving, move stock to the warehouse, 收货, 点货, 到货, 移到仓库.*

**Short answer:** open the voucher, go to its **At receiving gate** tab, open each package and write what
is inside, then press **Save changes**. If the gate is not a warehouse, move the products on with the
**Move to warehouse** tab. Products only become stock you can promise once they are counted at a warehouse.

## Steps

1. Open the voucher: **Suppliers**, pick the supplier, open the voucher. (Or on **Today**, in the **Receive**
   card, press **Count** on the shipment.)
2. If the supplier has sent the products but no shipment is recorded, go to the **On the way** tab first and
   press **Add shipment**: fill **Carrier**, **Packages (optional)**, **Shipment date** and **Receiving gate**,
   then press **Add shipment**.
3. Go to the **At receiving gate** tab. If it says nothing is counted yet, press **Start receiving**.
4. On the left is the list of packages: *Package #1*, *Package #2* and so on, each marked **Not counted**
   until you write something in it. **Total counted** at the bottom compares what you counted with what the
   voucher said.
5. Choose a package. Press **Add product** and fill the row:
   - **Product**: the stock code.
   - **Colors & quantities**: for example `black10s,white3s`.
   - If the next package holds the same, press **Copy previous**.
6. Do this for every package that arrived. A package can arrive on another day; count what is here now.
7. Press **Save changes** in the page header. Opening a package makes its contents real stock, and stock is
   allocated to waiting customer orders by itself, oldest order first.

## If the gate is not a warehouse

Products counted at a gate that is not a warehouse wait there; they are not stock you can promise yet.

1. Open the **Move to warehouse** tab, on **To move**.
2. Under **To** choose the warehouse and check the **Move date** (add a **Note (optional)** if you like).
   In the **Moving to the warehouse** column, press **Choose colors** on each product and pick what is moving.
   Then press **Move to warehouse**.
3. When the products reach the warehouse, open **To count**, press **Count what arrived**, check the
   **Count date** and the **Counted** quantity for each product. The **Difference** shows what is
   missing or extra.

## If the count does not match

- **Total counted** shows what you counted against what the voucher said. A different number means products
  are short or extra.
- A wrong count: open the package, change the numbers, press **Save changes**.
- A move by mistake: each move in the list has an undo button.

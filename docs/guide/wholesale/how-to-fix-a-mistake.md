# How do I fix a mistake or undo something?

*Other ways to ask: cancel an order, delete a voucher, I typed it wrong, undo, remove a payment, remove a
delivery, wrong quantity, 撤销, 取消订单, 删除单据, 改错了.*

**Short answer:** every delete, removal and cancellation shows a message for a few seconds with **Undo**.
Press it and nothing is lost. After that, the change is made. A wrong number on a saved record is changed
by editing it and pressing **Save changes**. **Save changes** saves everything you changed on the page, in every
tab, not only the tab you are looking at.

## Undo

After you delete or cancel something, a message appears at the bottom with **Undo**. You have about 8
seconds. There is no "are you sure?" question, so the message is your safety net.

## Cancel an order

1. Open the order (**Customers**, pick the customer, open the order).
2. Press the **•••** button (**Order actions**) at the top right and choose **Cancel order**.
3. The stock allocated to it goes back to available. Press **Undo** within a few seconds to bring the order back.

## Delete a voucher

1. Open the voucher (**Suppliers**, pick the supplier, open the voucher).
2. Press the **•••** button (**Voucher actions**) and choose **Delete voucher**.
3. You can only delete a voucher before any products were received on it and before any payment was recorded.
   Otherwise the choice is greyed out.

## Change a number on a saved record

- *A product line on an order or voucher*: edit the cells and press **Save changes**. To drop a line, press
  its remove icon (**Remove product**).
- *A payment*: open the **Payments** tab, change the **Date** or the amount, press **Save changes**, or remove
  the row with its remove icon.
- *A delivery*: open the order's **Deliveries** tab, then **Delivered**, and press the bin icon on the row.
- *A count at the gate*: open the voucher's **At receiving gate** tab, choose the package, change the
  numbers and press **Save changes**.
- *The customer or order details*: press **•••** (**Order actions**) and choose **Edit**. On a voucher choose
  **Edit voucher details**.

## If you leave halfway

If you leave a page with changes you have not saved, it asks "Leave without saving?". Stay if you still want
what you typed.

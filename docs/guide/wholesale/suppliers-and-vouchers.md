# Wholesale: suppliers and vouchers

## Where do I find what I ordered from a supplier?

Open **Suppliers** in the left menu and pick a supplier. The page shows what you owe them (**Open vouchers
total**, **Paid** and **Left to pay**) and their vouchers: *Open vouchers*, *Closed vouchers* and a list of
*Payments & transportation costs*. You can filter by voucher status and by payment status. From here you can start
a **New voucher** for this supplier.

A voucher is open until all its products are moved into a warehouse and it is paid in full.

## What does a voucher contain?

A voucher has a supplier, a date, the receiving gate the products will arrive at, and product lines. Each line
has a product, the colors and quantities, and a buying price per set. A voucher can also carry one **Supplier
discount** for the whole voucher. Two things are worked out for you: the stage and whether it is paid.

Open a voucher to see its tabs: **Ordered Items**, **On the way**, **At receiving gate**, **Move to warehouse**,
**Payments** and **Transportation cost**. They follow the products from the order to the warehouse.

## What does the supplier discount do?

Some suppliers give one discount on the whole voucher. Type it on the **Supplier discount** row under the
products, in the same three cells as a price: **Baht**, **Rate** and **Ks**. Type it in Baht and the rate is
filled in with today's rate and the Ks worked out; or type it in Ks if the supplier gave it in Ks. The voucher
total, and what you owe the supplier, go down by the discount. For cost and profit, the discount is shared over
the lines in proportion to their amounts, so each product's cost is what you really paid for it. A discount never
goes below zero owed.

## What do the voucher stages mean?

- On the way: ordered, and the products are still with the supplier or the carrier.
- At receiving gate: packages have reached the gate and some are counted, but not all.
- Counted: everything is counted, and nothing has been moved on yet.
- Partly moved: some of the counted products were moved on to a warehouse.
- Moved: everything is in a warehouse that has counted it, or was counted at a gate that is itself a warehouse.

Hover a Moved or Partly moved badge to see which place the products went to.

## What is "What to buy"?

On the **Suppliers** page, the **What to buy** page lists what customers are waiting for that nothing covers yet,
grouped by supplier. It looks at the stock you have, the stock that is already on its way, and what customers
ordered, product by product and color by color. You can add the lines straight into a new voucher.

## Can I change or delete a voucher?

You can change a voucher's lines and details and press **Save changes**. You cannot reduce a product below what
has already been received, and you can only delete a voucher before any products arrived on it and before any
payment was recorded. Deletes can be undone for a few seconds with **Undo**.

## How do I pay a supplier?

Add a row on the **Payments** tab: a date, an amount and a note. The voucher shows **Paid** and **Left to pay**.
A payment cannot be more than what is left to pay.

## Where do I see the totals in Baht?

When a product is priced in Baht, the voucher shows the Baht total under the Ks total (in the lines, the
discount rows and the total), and the Payments tab shows what you owe, have paid and still have to pay in
Baht too. Products or payments entered in Ks only are shown apart, for example "+ 500 Ks", because there is no
Baht amount to add up. The transportation cost total also shows its Baht part.

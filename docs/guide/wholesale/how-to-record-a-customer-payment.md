# How do I record a payment from a customer?

*Other ways to ask: customer paid, receive money, collect payment, add a payment, mark an order as
paid, left to collect, how much does the customer owe, 记录付款, 客户付款, 收款.*

**Short answer:** open the order, go to its **Payments** tab, press **Add payment**, fill the new row,
and press **Save changes**. The row is not saved until you press **Save changes**.

## Steps

1. Open the order. The quickest way is **Today**: in the **Money** card, stay on the **Collect** tab and
   press **Collect** on the customer. That opens the order on its **Payments** tab. Or press
   **Customers**, pick the customer and open the order, then press the **Payments** tab.
2. At the top of the tab, check **Total**, **Received** and **Left to collect**.
3. Press **Add payment**. A new row appears with today's date.
4. Fill the row:
   - **Date**: the day the money came in.
   - **Amount (Ks)**: the amount in Kyat.
   - If the customer paid in Baht, type the amount in **Amount (Baht)** and check the **Rate**: the
     Ks amount is worked out for you.
   - **Note** (optional): for example "cash" or "bank transfer".
5. Press **Save changes** in the page header. The toast says "Payments saved." and **Left to collect**
   goes down.

## Part payments and more than one payment

You can add a payment as soon as the order exists, before delivery. Each part payment is its own row.
The order shows **Partly paid** until **Left to collect** reaches zero, then **Paid**.

## If something is wrong

- *A payment is wrong*: change the Date or Amount in its row and press **Save changes**.
- *A payment should not be there*: press the bin icon on its row. **Undo** appears for a few seconds;
  after that it is removed.
- *A new row did not save*: a row is only saved when it has a date and an amount above zero.
- *The customer's total looks wrong*: **Customers** → the customer → **Payment history** lists every
  payment they have made.

## And for a supplier?

Paying a supplier is the same idea on the voucher: **Suppliers** → open the voucher → **Payments** tab →
**Add payment** → **Save changes**.

# How do I see what I should do now?

*Other ways to ask: what's next, what do I do today, my to-do list, what is waiting, what needs doing,
what should I deliver, what should I buy, who owes me, 今天要做什么, 待办, 下一步做什么.*

**Short answer:** open **Today**, the first item in the left menu. It is a to-do list in four cards. Press
the button on a row and it opens the right page.

## Steps

1. Press **Today**, the first item in the left menu.
2. **Deliver** card: orders with products allocated, ready to hand over. Press **Deliver** on a customer to
   open that order's delivery.
3. **Receive** card: what still has to be counted at the gate, and products moved to a warehouse that has not
   counted them yet. Press **Count** on either kind of row: "Count at" a gate for a shipment, or "Count at" a warehouse for moved products.
4. **Buy** card: what customers are waiting for that nothing is on the way for, by supplier. Press
   **What to buy** on a supplier to see the products, choose the ones you want and press
   **New voucher with selected** to order them in one go.
5. **Money** card: two tabs. **Collect** lists orders not fully paid, **Pay** lists supplier
   vouchers not fully paid, the biggest first. Press **Collect** or **Pay** to open the payments.

A card says what is empty in words, for example "No orders are ready to deliver."

## Two buttons at the top

- **New order**: a customer wants something (see "How do I record a customer order?").
- **New voucher**: you are ordering from a supplier (see "How do I record an order to a supplier?").

## Order to work in

Deliver what is ready, receive what arrived, buy what customers are waiting for, then take the money.
You can also ask the assistant "what should I do now?" and it will tell you.

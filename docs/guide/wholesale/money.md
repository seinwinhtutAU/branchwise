# Wholesale: who owes whom

## Where do I see what customers owe me?

- A customer's own page (**Customers**) shows **Open orders total**, **Received** and **Left to collect** for that
  customer, order by order.
- **Today** has a **Money** card. Its **Collect** tab lists orders that are not fully paid, largest first.
- The Dashboard's **Summary** and **Revenue** tabs show the total left to collect, and who owes the most.

**Left to collect** on an order is its total less what the customer has paid. It does not matter whether the order
has been delivered: an order delivered in full and still unpaid is counted, the same on every screen.

## Where do I see what I owe suppliers?

- A supplier's own page (**Suppliers**) shows **Open vouchers total**, **Paid** and **Left to pay**, voucher by voucher.
- **Today**'s **Money** card has a **Pay** tab listing vouchers that are not fully paid, largest first.
- The Dashboard's **Summary** and **Cost** tabs show the total left to pay.

**Left to pay** on a voucher is its total after any supplier discount, less what you have paid.

## How do payments work?

A payment is a row with a date, an amount and a note. A customer or a supplier can be paid in several parts.

- A payment must be more than zero.
- What has been received (or paid) can never be more than the total of the order (or voucher).
- A payment can be typed in Baht with a rate; the Ks amount follows. The rate on a saved payment never changes later.
- You can change or remove a payment on its **Payments** tab and press **Save changes**.

An order or voucher shows Not paid, Partly paid or Paid, from its payments.

## How old is the money owed?

The Dashboard shows what is left to collect, and what is left to pay, by how old the order or voucher is: up to
7 days, 8 to 30 days and over 30 days.

# How do I record a payment to a supplier?

*Other ways to ask: I paid the supplier, pay a voucher, record a supplier payment, how much do I owe,
left to pay, pay the factory, 付款给供应商, 记录供应商付款, 欠供应商多少.*

**Short answer:** open the voucher, go to its **Payments** tab, press **Add payment**, fill the new row,
and press **Save changes**. The row is not saved until you press **Save changes**.

## Steps

1. Open the voucher. The quickest way is **Today**: in the **Money** card open the **Pay** tab and press
   **Pay** on the supplier. Or press **Suppliers**, pick the supplier and open the voucher, then open
   the **Payments** tab.
2. At the top of the tab, check **Total**, **Paid** and **Left to pay**.
3. Press **Add payment**. A new row appears.
4. Fill the row:
   - **Date**: the day you paid.
   - **Amount (Ks)**: what you paid in Kyat.
   - If you paid in Baht, type it in **Amount (Baht)** and check the **Rate**: the Ks amount is worked
     out for you.
   - **Note** (optional): for example "bank transfer".
5. Press **Save changes** in the page header. **Left to pay** goes down.

## Part payments

Each part payment is its own row. The voucher shows **Partly paid** until **Left to pay** reaches zero,
then **Paid**.

## If something is wrong

- *A payment is wrong*: change the **Date** or the amount in its row and press **Save changes**.
- *A payment should not be there*: press the remove icon on its row. **Undo** appears for a few seconds;
  after that it is removed.
- *A new row did not save*: a row is only saved when it has a date and an amount above zero.
- A voucher with a payment recorded can no longer be deleted.

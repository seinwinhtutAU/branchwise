# How do I record an order to a supplier (a voucher)?

*Other ways to ask: new voucher, order from the supplier, buy stock, add a purchase, enter a supplier
voucher, I ordered shoes from the factory, 录入供应商单据, 新建单据, 向供应商订货.*

**Short answer:** press **New voucher**, fill in three short steps (the supplier, the products with their
buying prices, a check), and press **Save voucher**. The shipment, the counting and the payments come
later, on the voucher itself.

## Steps

1. Press **New voucher**. You find it on **Today**, on a supplier's page under **Suppliers**, or on a
   product's page under **Products**. From a supplier's page the supplier is already filled in.
2. **Supplier and carrier.** Check the **Voucher date**. Choose or type the **Supplier**. Choose the
   **Receiving gate** the products will arrive at. If you already know it, fill **Carrier (optional)** and
   **Packages (optional)**. Press **Next**.
3. **Products.** Press **Add product** and fill one row for each product:
   - **Product**: the stock code or name.
   - **Colors & quantities**: colors with a unit letter and no spaces, for example `black10s,pink2p`
     (10 sets of black and 2 pairs of pink). `p` is pairs, `s` is sets, `d` is dozens.
   - **Buying price (Ks)**: the price for one set. If the supplier quotes in Baht, fill **Buying price
     (Baht)** and the **Rate**, and the Ks figure follows.

   To order what customers are already waiting for, press **Add what customers are waiting for**
   (choose the supplier first). Press **Next**.
4. **Review and confirm.** Check the **Voucher number** and the **Voucher total**. The table shows each
   product and the totals in Baht and in Ks, with the supplier discount taken off. Press **Save voucher**.

   If the supplier gives a discount on the whole voucher, type it on the previous step, on the
   **Supplier discount** row under the products, in the same Baht, Rate and Ks cells as a price (type it in
   Baht and today's rate is filled in). On a voucher you have saved, type it in that row, or use **•••**,
   **Edit voucher details**. Leave it at 0 for no discount. The total, and what you owe the supplier, go down
   by the discount.

A message says "Voucher ... created." and you go back to where you started. If you filled in the
carrier or the packages, the shipment is added with it. Otherwise open the voucher later and use its
**On the way** tab (**Add shipment**) when the supplier sends the products.

## If something is refused

- *"Enter a supplier name."* The supplier is empty.
- *"Say which receiving gate the products go to."* A carrier or packages were filled in, so the gate is needed.
- *"Add at least one product."* There is no product row.
- *"Enter valid color quantities, such as black10s or pink2p."* Write colors with a unit letter and no
  spaces.
- *"Choose a stock code."* or *"Enter a product description."* A product row is not finished.
- *Left halfway*: the page asks "Leave without saving?". Stay if you still want what you typed.

## Afterwards

- Change anything: open the voucher, edit the cells, press **Save changes**.
- The products are sent: **On the way** tab. They arrive: **At receiving gate** tab. Pay the supplier:
  **Payments** tab.

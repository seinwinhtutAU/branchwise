# How do I record a customer order?

*Other ways to ask: new order, add an order, customer wants to buy, enter an order, create a sale,
customer rang me, 录入订单, 新订单, 客户下单.*

**Short answer:** press **New order**, fill in three short steps (the customer, the products, a check),
and press **Save order**. Delivery and payment are recorded later, on the order itself.

## Steps

1. Press **New order**. You find it on **Today**, or on a customer's page under **Customers**, or on a
   product's page under **Products**. From a customer's page the customer is already filled in.
2. **Customer information.** Check the **Order date**. Choose or type the **Customer name**. If the
   customer is already known, the **Phone number** and **Address** fill themselves in. Press **Next**.
3. **Products.** Press **Add product** and fill one row for each product:
   - **Product**: the stock code or name.
   - **Colors & quantities**: type the colors with a unit letter and no spaces, for example
     `black10s,pink2p` (10 sets of black and 2 pairs of pink). `p` is pairs, `s` is sets, `d` is dozens.
     You can also pick the colors from the list.
   - **Selling price (Ks)**: the price for one set. When you click it to type, a small popup suggests
     a price: the product's cost (its buying price after the supplier's discount, plus the transport
     cost of the same supplier voucher) with the profit from Settings (Wholesale, Selling price
     profit) added. Press **Use this price** to take it, or type your own. If you quote in Baht, type
     it in **Selling price (Baht)** and the **Rate**, and the Ks figure follows (no suggestion is shown
     for a price in Baht).
   Press **Next**.
4. **Review and confirm.** Check the customer, the products and the **Total**. Press **Save order**.

A message says "Order ... created." and you go back to where you started. Open the order again from
**Customers** to see it. If stock is already on the shelf, it is allocated to this order straight away;
otherwise the order shows **Waiting for stock** and stock is allocated as it arrives.

## If something is refused

- **"Enter a customer name."** The customer name is empty.
- **"Enter at least one color and quantity."** A product row has no colors. Write them like
  `black10s`.
- **"Enter a selling price."** A product row has no price.
- *A product twice*: one row per product. Put the colors for that product on one row.
- *Left halfway*: the page asks "Leave without saving?". Stay if you still want what you typed.

## Afterwards

- Change anything on the order: open it, edit the cells, press **Save changes**.
- Wrong order: open it, press the **•••** button (Order actions) and choose **Cancel order**. Its stock
  goes back to available. **Undo** appears for a few seconds.
- Record the delivery on the **Deliveries** tab and the money on the **Payments** tab.

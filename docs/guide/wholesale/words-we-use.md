# Wholesale: what the words mean

Every word below is written the way the screens write it. If someone uses another word for the same thing,
the right-hand column says which screen word they mean.

## People and papers

| On the screen | What it is | Other words people use |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier | Who you buy products from | factory, maker |
| Customer | Who you sell to | buyer, shop |
| Voucher | What you ordered from a supplier: products, quantities and buying prices | purchase, purchase order, your order |
| Order | What a customer ordered from you: products, quantities and selling prices | sale, customer order |
| Shipment | What the carrier sends along from the supplier to your gate | slip, freight, consignment |
| Carrier | Who carries the shipment: a cargo company or a driver | cargo, cargo company |
| Package | One carton of products, as it arrives | box, carton |
| Product | One thing you sell, with a stock code and a product group | model, item |
| Stock code | The product's code | code, SKU |
| Product group | Man, Lady, Child or Other | type, category |

## Places and stock

| On the screen | What it means |
|---|---|
| Place | Anywhere products are kept. A receiving gate and a warehouse are both places |
| Receiving gate | Where packages arrive and are counted. Only a gate that is also a warehouse holds stock you can promise |
| Warehouse | A place that holds stock. Products counted at a warehouse are stock at once |
| Stock | Products counted at a warehouse |
| Allocated | Stock kept for one customer's order. It stays allocated until it is delivered or the order is cancelled |
| Available | What a warehouse holds, less what is allocated. This is what you can still promise to a new order |
| Incoming | Stock that is still on its way |
| Move | Taking counted products from one place to another. The place they go to counts them again |
| Short, Extra | The count is lower or higher than what was expected. The screen asks why |

## Quantities, colors and prices

- 6 pairs make 1 set. 12 pairs make 1 dozen. Quantities are shown as sets, with any leftover pairs.
- Prices are quoted per set. The currency is Myanmar Kyat (Ks). A line can also be priced in Baht with
  a rate; the Ks amount follows.
- Colors are written with the number and a unit letter, no spaces: `black10s,pink2p` is 10 sets of black
  and 2 pairs of pink. `p` is pairs, `s` is sets, `d` is dozens.
- Buying price is on a voucher. Selling price is on an order.

## Money

| On the screen | What it means |
|---|---|
| Received | Money a customer has paid you |
| Left to collect | What a customer still owes you |
| Paid | Money you have paid a supplier |
| Left to pay | What you still owe a supplier |
| Not paid, Partly paid, Paid | How much of the total has been paid |
| Transportation cost | What it cost to get the products in: carrier fee, porters, gate handling, a second trip |

## Stages

- Order: Waiting for stock, then Ready to deliver, then Partly delivered, then Delivered. An order can also be Cancelled.
- Voucher: On the way, then At receiving gate, then Counted, then Partly moved, then Moved.
- Shipment: At carrier, On the way, Partly arrived, Arrived.

## Buttons

- **New** starts a whole record: New order, New voucher.
- **Add** adds a row inside a table: Add product, Add payment.
- **Record** opens a page to write down something that happened: Record delivery.
- **Delete** removes a whole record and **Remove** takes out a row. Both can be undone for a few seconds.

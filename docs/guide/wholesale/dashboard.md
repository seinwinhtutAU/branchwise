# Wholesale: the Dashboard

## What is the Dashboard for?

It answers a few questions without opening each record: are we selling more or less than before, who owes us,
what do we owe, is the stock where it should be, and are customers coming back. It has six tabs: **Summary**,
**Health**, **Revenue**, **Cost**, **Inventory** and **Customer**. At the top right, **Export to Excel** and
**CSV files** download your data.

The period control (daily, weekly, monthly, or From and to dates) sets which days the tabs look at. Each figure is
compared with the period right before it, of the same length. A dash means the figure could not be worked out; it
is never shown as zero.

## What is on the Summary tab?

A switch at the top chooses **Open now** or **By period**.

- **Open now**: what is still open right now. *Left to collect* shows the **Open orders total** and what is
  **Received**, with **View unpaid orders**. *Left to pay* shows the **Open vouchers total** and what is **Paid**,
  with **View unpaid vouchers**.
- **By period**: three columns. *Orders* has the **Orders total**, **Received** and **Left to collect**. *Cost*
  has the **Total cost**, **Paid**, **Left to pay** and **Gross profit**. *Customers* has the **Customers who
  ordered**, **New customers** and **Repeat customers**. Click a column's title to open its tab.

Under both sits the **Stock** board, which always shows how things stand now: every set of stock from the supplier
to your warehouses. It splits the stock into At supplier (ordered, not sent), On the way (sent, not arrived), At
receiving gate (counted, not moved) and In a warehouse (counted and moved in), and splits what is in a warehouse
into Allocated and Available. Click a stage to see those products.

## What is on the Revenue tab?

A sentence at the top says in words how sales did. Then **Orders total** (what customers ordered in the period),
**Received** and **Left to collect**, a chart of the orders placed each day against the period before, and the
share each product group has. Lists show the top customers and the top products by order value. Below, what is
still open: the customers who owe the most, and what is left to collect by how old the orders are.

Revenue here means orders placed, not products delivered.

## What is on the Cost tab?

**Total cost** is what was bought in the period. Next to it are the profit figures: **Gross profit**, **Gross margin**
and **Net profit**, for the products that were delivered in the period. Gross profit is delivered sales less
what those products cost. Net profit also takes off their transportation cost. A dash, with the reason under it,
means some delivered product has no supplier voucher to take a cost from. The **Cost breakdown** splits the
period's spending into Products bought and Transportation cost. **Profit by day** compares each day's net profit
with the period before. The lists show the top suppliers and the top products by purchase value, and what is
still open on vouchers.

## What is on the Inventory tab?

It shows the stock as it is now and does not follow the period. **Available** is what can still be promised.
**Allocated** is held for open orders. **Incoming** is still on its way. **Stock value** is what the stock cost.
Below are *Stock by place* (receiving gates and warehouses) and *Stock by product group*, with what each cost.

## What is on the Customer tab?

How many customers ordered (**Customers who ordered**), how many were new and how many came back, and what each
group ordered. Charts show the orders by day, the customers by month and *How often customers buy*. *Who has not
ordered for a while* lists customers who have gone quiet beyond their own usual gap. A card shows where the
open orders are on their way to the customer: Waiting for stock, Ready to deliver, Partly delivered, Delivered.

## What is the Health tab?

One score from 0 to 100 for the wholesale business, in four parts: Orders, Money, Inventory and Profit. 80 and
over is Healthy, 60 to 79 Needs attention, and below 60 Critical. See how the score is worked out in "wholesale:
how every number is worked out (index)". The owner can change the weights.

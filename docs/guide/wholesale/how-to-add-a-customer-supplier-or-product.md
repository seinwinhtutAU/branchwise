# How do I add a new customer, supplier or product?

*Other ways to ask: new customer, add a supplier, create a product, register a stock code, add a new shoe,
新客户, 新增供应商, 新产品, 添加货号.*

**Short answer:** open the list (**Customers**, **Suppliers** or **Products**), press **New**, fill the
short form and press **Save customer**, **Save supplier** or **Save product**.

## A customer

1. Press **Customers** in the left menu, then **New** above the list.
2. Fill **Customer name** (required). **Phone number** and **Address** are optional.
3. Press **Save customer**.

## A supplier

1. Press **Suppliers**, then **New**.
2. Fill **Supplier name** (required), and the phone and address if you have them.
3. Press **Save supplier**.

## A product

1. Press **Products**, then **New**.
2. Under **Product identification** fill **Stock code**, **Description** and **Product group**.
3. Under **Pricing**, **Default selling price** and **Default buying price** are only suggestions: they
   pre-fill a new order or voucher line, which always keeps its own price after that.
4. Press **Save product**.

## Change one later

Open the customer, supplier or product and use its edit button. The form is the same, and it ends with
**Save**.

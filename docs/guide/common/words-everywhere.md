# Words that mean the same everywhere

- The currency is Myanmar Kyat, written **Ks**.
- Retail and wholesale are two separate businesses. A retail branch's sales never mix with the wholesale business's
  orders. The wholesale business has its own words: see "Wholesale: what the words mean".
- **Branch**: one shop. The retail branches each have their own data. The wholesale business is one more branch.
- A figure that cannot be worked out is shown as a dash. It is never shown as 0.
- Changes in a figure are written as a plain %. The comparison says what it is compared with: the period before,
  or the same dates last year.
- **Critical**, **Warning** and **Normal** are how serious an alert is. Critical needs a decision now.

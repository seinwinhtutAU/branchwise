# Using the assistant, Explain and Knowledge

## What is the assistant?

A chat helper for admin accounts. Open it with the floating button at the bottom of the screen. Ask in plain
words, in English, Burmese or Chinese. It looks the answer up in your own data: sales, cost, stock, alerts,
imports and, for wholesale, orders, vouchers, shipments and money. It does not change anything, and it says when it
could not find something. Every answer can show where its figures came from, and you can mark an answer right or wrong.

## What can I ask?

- How a business is doing: "how were sales yesterday?", "is the wholesale business healthy?", "why is the margin down?"
- What to do: "what should I decide first?", "what should I do now?", "what is running low?", "what is waiting for stock?". The assistant puts the Business Alerts and the wholesale to-do lists in one order, most important first, with the reason and what to do for each. It only suggests; you decide. It remembers what it listed: next time it says how long each one has been waiting and what has cleared since, and when you open the chat a note "From last time" shows what is still waiting. Say "snooze #12 for 3 days" to put one off, or "I won't do #12" to stop it being listed. A decision counts as cleared when the app's own alert or to-do list stops raising it. Ask for the exact products to see them: which slow stock to move to which branch, which best sellers to reorder and how many, what to order from which supplier and about what it will cost, which customers to ask for money first (the ones whose money is more than 30 days old, then the largest balances), and what price to sell a product for at several profit levels.
- Money: "who owes me the most?", "what do I owe suppliers?"
- How to do something: "how do I record a payment?", "how do I fix a wrong import?"
- What a word or a number means: "how is the health score worked out?"
- What a setting is today: it reads the value in use, so it does not give an old number.
- A chart: "show me revenue by branch".

## What is Explain?

Press **Explain** at the top of the screen, then click a number, a chart, a column heading or a row. A small bubble
opens beside it and says in two or three plain sentences what it means and why it is what it is. Pick Ask more to
carry on in the chat. Explain switches off when you press it again.

## What is the Knowledge page?

The assistant also reads the **Knowledge** page. It holds these written guides, and notes you write yourself:
rules, terms and how-tos for your business. A note is used only when it is Published. A draft is ignored. If your note
and a guide disagree, your note wins, and the assistant says which it used. After the guides change, press **Sync Knowledge** so the assistant reads the new text. To write your own, press **Add information**.
